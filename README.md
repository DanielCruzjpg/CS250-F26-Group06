Our Group’s Purpose

Our group agrees to work respectfully, responsibly, and cooperatively. We will support one another, communicate clearly, and complete our work so that every member contributes to the group’s success.

Four Ground Rules
1. Communicate respectfully: We will listen to each other’s ideas, avoid interrupting, and give constructive feedback.
2. Participate equally: Every member will contribute meaningful work and stay involved in group decisions.
3. Be dependable: We will complete our assigned tasks by the agreed deadlines.
4. Be honest and responsible: We will inform the group early if we are confused, unavailable, or unable to finish an assignment.

Problems We Want to Prevent
1.Unequal participation: To prevent one person from doing most of the work, we will divide assignments clearly and check in regularly.
2.Poor communication: To prevent missed information, we will use one main communication method and share important updates with the entire group.
3.Last-minute or incomplete work: To prevent rushed work, we will set earlier internal deadlines and review each person’s work before the final submission.

How We Function Best as a Group
*We function best in groups when everyone has a clear responsibility.
*We function best when members communicate openly and respectfully.
*We function best when we have enough time to ask questions, make changes, and combine our work.
*We function best when all members feel comfortable sharing their ideas.

We really hope our group can work together successfully, maintain a positive attitude, and produce high-quality work that reflects everyone’s contributions.

Division of Work

Work will be divided based on the assignment requirements, each member’s strengths, and individual availability. Responsibilities will be discussed and recorded before work begins.

Each member is responsible for:

*Completing their assigned portion.
*Meeting the agreed-upon deadline.
*Asking questions when clarification is needed.
*Reviewing the completed group project.
*Helping the group when reasonably possible.

If the assignment requires a specific role, the group may assign roles such as researcher, writer, editor, designer, or presenter. Roles may be changed if the group agrees.

Communication

Our primary communication method will be:

Communication platform: instagram

Members should use the group communication method for important updates, questions, shared files, and deadline reminders. We will communicate respectfully and avoid sending unnecessary messages late at night unless the matter is urgent.

Expected Response Times

Group members should respond to messages within 24 hours on weekdays and within 48 hours on weekends, whenever possible. If someone cannot respond within that time, they should notify the group when they are available.

Deadlines

The group will create internal deadlines before the instructor’s final deadline. Members will complete their work by the agreed internal deadline so the group has time to combine, review, and revise the project.

If Someone Cannot Complete Their Work

If a member cannot complete an assigned task, they must notify the group as soon as possible. The group will discuss how to divide or adjust the unfinished work. The member should provide any work they have already completed and help the group find a solution.

If a member repeatedly misses deadlines or does not communicate, the group may document the situation and inform the instructor.

Resolving Disagreements

When disagreements occur, we will:

1. Allow each person to explain their viewpoint.
2. Focus on the assignment rather than criticizing individuals.
3. Use evidence, assignment instructions, and group goals to make decisions.
4. Vote or reach a compromise if we cannot agree.
5. Ask the instructor for guidance if the disagreement cannot be resolved.
   
Meetings Outside of Class

Meeting method: Campus/class
Usual meeting days/times: twice a day, after class

Members who cannot attend a meeting should notify the group beforehand and complete any assigned follow-up work.
